# 🐱 Cat Conqueror

A side-scrolling 2D platformer built with **JavaScript** and **p5.js** for the University of London *Introduction to Programming* module.

### ▶ [Play here](https://abdulla-hiyaz.github.io/UoL-ITP-Cat-Conqueror-Game/)

## How to play
- **Arrow keys** – move and jump
- **Jump on enemies** from above to squash them
- **Collect all 10 orbs** to complete the level
- You have **3 lives**; avoid canyons and enemy contact

## Features
- Gravity-based jump physics and platform collision
- Scrolling camera that follows the player
- Patrolling enemies with a stomp mechanic
- Start, game over and level complete screens
- Sound effects synthesised with p5.sound
